## Design Context

### Users
Dana Gorbahn's family (children, grandchildren, extended family) reading his church
talks, Christmas letters, and family photos/videos as a personal keepsake and
devotional archive. Not a public/marketing audience — this is a private family book
put online for convenience.

### Brand Personality
Warm, personal, literary, unpretentious. Should read as a real book (title page +
table of contents), never as a SaaS/startup landing page. Explicitly rejected on
first attempt: rounded pill CTA buttons with arrow icons, uppercase "eyebrow" micro
labels used as marketing copy, stat-tile rows (75/14/8 as a metrics block), and
em dashes in any copy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the site's existing look exactly as-is: Inter typeface, pure black ink (#111111)
on pure white, `--sidebar-bg` #f4f4f4, `--border` #dddddd. No new fonts, no new
colors, no warm/cream paper tone (explicitly declined). The "book" feeling should
come from typographic structure and restraint, not decoration — real book table-
of-contents conventions (leader lines between title and date, chapter-index-style
multi-column lists) rather than web-app patterns (cards, buttons, badges, icons).
Anti-references: SaaS landing pages, AI-generic marketing page templates.

### Design Principles
1. Church Talks (75 items) is the primary content of any contents/index view —
   laid out as its own multi-column chapter index, not squeezed evenly beside the
   other two sections.
2. Christmas Letters and More Stuff are secondary — positioned below Church Talks,
   not side-by-side with it.
3. No em dashes anywhere in copy. Keep language plain and simple.
4. Borrow structure from real book front matter (title page, table of contents),
   not from web marketing patterns. No pill buttons, no eyebrow-label-plus-CTA
   combos, no stat-tile rows.
5. Reuse the exact type scale and color tokens already defined in `index.html`.
   Do not introduce new fonts or a new palette.
